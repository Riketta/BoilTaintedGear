# Boil Tainted Gear

Stoves and campfires gain a "boil tainted gear" bill that washes corpse taint out of apparel. Anything that can become tainted shows up in the bill filter as usual; the cleaned item keeps quality, color and style.
Costs 30% of the item's current durability by default (configurable), never destroys the item.

## What it does

- Adds a "boil tainted gear" bill to electric stoves, fueled stoves and campfires - no research needed, tribal-friendly.
- Accepts tainted apparel from any mod or DLC; untainted pieces are never boiled.
- The cleaned item is placed next to the fire for haulers to carry back.
- Durability cost, debug logging and a master switch live in the mod settings.

No dependencies. Works with all DLC and mods by design, safe to add or remove at any time.
